How to interpret these results

Run or edit a model to see result-specific interpretation. Computed statistics describe sample associations, not proof of causation. Live preview is provisional; validate the estimator against established software.

Understanding every SEM-PLS calculation

Path coefficient (β)

Direction and relative strength of a modeled relationship; sign and size depend on scaling and specification.

Bootstrap CI / p-value

Sampling uncertainty under resampling assumptions. A CI crossing zero does not support an unambiguous nonzero effect at that level.

R²

Explained variance of an endogenous construct in this sample. Not evidence of generalizability or causation.

f²

Change in explained variance when a predictor is omitted, conditional on the estimated model.

Outer loading / weight

Relationship of indicators to constructs; reflective loadings and formative weights have different interpretations.

Cronbach α and composite reliability

Internal-consistency metrics for reflective multi-item constructs. Neither alone proves unidimensionality.

AVE

Average variance extracted by the reflective construct; routinely compared with 0.50 as a screening heuristic.

HTMT / Fornell–Larcker

Discriminant-validity diagnostics, not definitive evidence of construct distinctness.

VIF

Indicates possible collinearity among predictors or formative indicators; inspect high values contextually.

Indirect effect

Product of paths representing an estimated mediated association; a bootstrap interval is more informative than the sign alone.

OLS regression

Additional exploratory linear model with classical errors; its assumptions differ from PLS-SEM.

Descriptive statistics

Distribution of observed dataset values, including mean, standard deviation, median and missingness.
